## 📌 Project Overview
Analyzing large-scale real estate transactions requires robust data processing to handle high transaction volumes, diverse property classifications, and wide geographical price distributions.

This project delivers a multi-page executive BI suite processing over **1.05M+ real estate records**, evaluating over **₦418Bn in total sales revenue** across town performances, property type distributions, and price dynamics.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Objectives
* **Market Valuation & Revenue Audit:** Track total sales volume, average price per square foot, and town-level revenue performance across 1M+ transactions.
* **Property Portfolio Segmentation:** Analyze demand and sales distributions across Single-Family, Multi-Family, Commercial, and Condo properties.
* **Geographical Performance Mapping:** Identify high-value real estate hotspots and emerging affordable housing corridors.
* **Pricing Dynamics & Appreciation:** Evaluate time-on-market metrics and historical sales price growth to uncover investment potential.

---

## 🧹 Data Pipeline & Architecture
* **High-Volume Data Migration:** Successfully migrated a dataset exceeding **1,000,000+ rows** from Microsoft Excel into Power BI to overcome system RAM and performance limits.
* **ETL & Data Transformation:** Applied Power Query to standardize property type names, clean null valuation fields, and parse transaction dates into a structured calendar dimension.
* **Data Modeling:** Designed an optimized star-schema model with explicit DAX measures for **Total Valuation**, **Average Sales Price**, **YoY Price Variance**, and **Property Type Market Share**.

---

## 💡 Key Market Insights
* **Residential Dominance:** Single-Family homes account for the majority of sales volume, while Commercial properties yield the highest average margin per transaction.
* **Town-Level Concentration:** Top-performing towns generate over 40% of total regional revenue, indicating strong geographic buyer demand.
* **Price Range Elasticity:** Properties in mid-tier pricing brackets demonstrate the fastest absorption rates compared to luxury estate categories.

---
