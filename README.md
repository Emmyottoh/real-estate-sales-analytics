# Executive Real Estate Performance & Revenue Analytics Suite

## Project Overview
Real estate executives require high-level commercial visibility and granular market benchmarking to evaluate property sales performance, monitor total revenue trends, and identify top-performing geographic markets[cite: 1]. This project delivers a multi-page interactive Power BI analytics suite engineered to process and visualize 1,000,000+ transaction records across diverse property categories[cite: 1].

## Technical Stack & Tools
* **Business Intelligence & Visualization:** Power BI Desktop, Interactive Dashboard Design, Advanced DAX (Calculated Columns & Measures)[cite: 1]
* **Data Engineering & ETL:** SQL, Power Query, Data Cleaning, Normalization, Data Quality Auditing[cite: 1, 2]
* **Data Modeling & Architecture:** Relational Star Schema, Date Hierarchy Alignment[cite: 1]
* **Core Competencies:** Analytical Skills, Statistics, Data Analytics, Revenue Benchmarking, Executive Communication[cite: 1, 2]

## Key Features & Business Impact
* **Large-Scale Data Architecture:** Engineered robust ETL pipelines to ingest, clean, and normalize 1,000,000+ real estate transaction records, resolving legacy timeline default errors and date hierarchy mismatches[cite: 1].
* **Executive Revenue Tracking:** Built an executive summary dashboard monitoring ₦418 Billion in total sales revenue and 1.05 Million properties sold[cite: 1].
* **Granular Market Benchmarking:** Designed town-level price benchmarks and an interactive Town vs. Category revenue matrix to isolate high-value markets (e.g., Greenwich and Stamford)[cite: 1].
* **Statistical Sales Analysis:** Applied descriptive **Statistics** and trend modeling to evaluate sales trajectory, price distribution, and volume fluctuations over time[cite: 1].

## Data Pipeline & Methodology
1. **ETL & Data Cleaning:** Applied **Power Query** and **SQL** transformations to correct schema shifts, standardize pricing columns, and remove record anomalies[cite: 1, 2].
2. **Data Modeling:** Built a relational **Data Modeling** structure connecting property transactions, calendar tables, and location lookups with optimized DAX performance[cite: 1].
3. **Interactive Reporting:** Implemented cross-filtering, dynamic slicers, and town benchmarking matrices to facilitate clear **Communication** with executive stakeholders[cite: 1, 2].
